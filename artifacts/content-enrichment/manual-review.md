## False positives removed

- A chapter-wide ilal title previously labelled examples from its separate ibdal section. Rule evidence now comes from the specific example section or explicit grammatical roles.
- The ordinary nominal sentence `لَوْنُ الْوَرْدَةِ جَمِيلٌ` previously inherited the chapter’s governing-adjective topic. Chapter titles now supply provider context only. They cannot independently create that candidate.
- An ibdal/idgham section with ambiguous combined rule evidence is deferred to contextual analysis. A single author title cannot decide which of several operations the example illustrates.
- Arabic inside IDs, URLs, references, answer keys, English explanations, diagrams, and incomplete lexical alternatives is excluded by explicit adapters.
