## Grammar Points

| Grammar Point | Explanation | Example from Article |
|---|---|---|
| **Future tense (سَ + present)** | Add سَ or سَوْفَ before present verb for future | **سَيَتَلَقَّى** النَّاسُ تَدْرِيبًا (People will receive training) |
| **Definite article الـ** | Add الـ to make noun specific/definite | **الذَّكَاءُ** الاصْطِنَاعِيُّ (The artificial intelligence) |
| **Conjunctions: وَ, لكن, أن** | Connect sentences and clauses | ...**وَلَكِنَّكَ** تَحْصُلُ عَلَى جَائِزَةٍ (...but you obtain a certificate) |
| **Reported speech: قال أنَّ** | "He said that..." to report someone's words | قَالَ جَامِس مَانِيكَا: "الذَّكَاءُ يُقَدِّمُ فُرَصًا" |
| **Relative clause: الذي/التي** | Use to describe nouns (implied here) | الدَّوْرَةُ **الَّتِي** تَدُومُ عَشَرَ سَاعَاتٍ |
| **Prepositions: فِي, عَلَى, مِنْ, بَعْدَ, إِلَى** | Show relationships between words | فِي مَهَارَاتِ الذَّكَاءِ, عَلَى جَائِزَةٍ, بَعْدَ الدَّوْرَةِ |
| **Verb + هُ/هَا (possessive suffix)** | Attach pronoun to end of verb/noun | تَحْسَنَتْ **إِنْتَاجِيَّتُهُمْ** (their productivity improved) |
| **لا + noun = negation of existence** | "There is no..." or "No...needed" | **لَا حَاجَةَ** إِلَى شَهَادَةٍ (No degree needed) |
| **Percentage expression** | Number + % + مِنْ | **٨٦٪ مِنَ** النَّاسِ (86% of people) |
| **Idaafa structure (possessive chain)** | Two or more nouns in a row | مَهَارَاتِ **الذَّكَاءِ الاصْطِنَاعِيِّ** (skills of artificial intelligence) |
| **Comparative: أَكْثَرُ مِنْ** | "More than..." with أَفْعَلُ form | **أَكْثَرَ مِنْ** مِلْيُونِ إِنْسَانٍ (more than one million people) |
| **Passive participle (مَفْعُول)** | Used as adjective from verb | التَّدْرِيبُ **الْمَجَّانِيُّ** (the free training) |
