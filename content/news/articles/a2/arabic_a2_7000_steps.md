## Grammar Points

| Grammar Point | Explanation | Example from Text |
|---|---|---|
| **Verb + إنَّ + accusative** | After verbs of saying/thinking, إِنَّ introduces a clause and puts the predicate in accusative case (منصوب) | قَالَتْ دِينْغ إِنَّ **الحَقِيقَةَ** مُهِمَّةٌ |
| **Verb + أَنَّ + accusative** | Similar to إِنَّ, used after confirming verbs; subject becomes accusative | تُؤَكِّدُ أَنَّ **الحَرَكَةَ** تُحَسِّنُ |
| **Definite Adjective Agreement** | Adjective matches noun in gender, number, definiteness, and case | الدِّرَاسَةُ **الجَدِيدَةُ**, الإِرْشَادَاتُ **الصِّحِّيَّةُ** |
| **Numbers 3-10: Reversed Agreement** | Numbers 3-10 show "reversed" gender agreement (feminine number with masculine noun) | سَبْعَةَ (m) **آلَافِ** (m pl), عَشَرَةِ (m) **آلَافٍ** (m pl) |
| **Form II Verb Pattern (تَفْعِيل)** | Doubled middle root letter; often causative or intensive meaning | تُؤَكِّدُ (تُـ + أَكِّدُ), تُحَسِّنُ, تُقَلِّلُ, تُحَدِّثُ |
| **Form VIII Verb Pattern (اِفْتِعَال)** | Added ت after first root letter; often reflexive or participative | يَسْتَفِيدُ (he benefits), يَشْتَرِكُ (he participates), يَسْتَطِيعُ |
| **Reported Speech with قَالَ** | Direct quote introduced by colon in Arabic text | قَالَتْ دِينْغ: "**سَبْعَةُ آلَافٍ هَدَفٌ عَمَلِيٌّ**" |
| **The Appositive (Badal)** | A noun that renames/clarifies another noun; matches in case | شَارَكَ **مِائَةٌ وَسِتُّونَ أَلْفَ بَالِغٍ** (the number appositively describes participants) |
| **Conjunctions: وَ, لَكِنْ, لِذَلِكَ** | Connecting words for compound sentences | **وَ**الفَائِدَةُ, **وَ**تُحَدِّثُ |
| **Nisba Adjective (يَاءُ النِّسْبَةِ)** | Adjective formed by adding ـِيّ to a noun | يَابَانِيٌّ (from اليَابَانُ), عِلْمِيَّةٌ (from العِلْمُ) |
| **Genitive Chain (إِضَافَةٌ)** | Possessive construction: noun + noun in genitive | مَجَلَّةُ **لَانْسِت**, هَدَفٌ **عَمَلِيٌّ**, نَتَائِجُ **صِحَّةِ الإِنْسَانِ** |
