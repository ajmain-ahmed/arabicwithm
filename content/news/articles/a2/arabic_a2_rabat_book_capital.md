**Grammar Notes — Paragraph 1:**
- أَخْبَرَتْ: feminine past tense (she announced/informed) — feminine form of أَخْبَرَ; takes two objects (نَبَأً = the news announced)
- قَالَتْ: feminine past tense (she said) — feminine form of قَالَ
- بِهَذَا: preposition بِـ + demonstrative هَذَا — "with this"
- الِاخْتِيَارِ: verbal noun (مَصْدَر) of اخْتَارَ, form VIII of خ-ي-ر — "choice, selection"; after بِـ it takes genitive case (مَجْرُور)
- الْحَادِيَةُ وَالْعِشْرُونَ: ordinal number "twenty-first" — feminine form of الْحَادِي وَالْعِشْرُونَ
- تَقْلِيدٌ: verbal noun (مَصْدَر) of قَلَّدَ — "tradition"; indefinite (نَكِرَة) predicate of nominal sentence
- فِكْرَةٌ: derived from ف-ك-ر — "idea"; indefinite (نَكِرَة) subject of nominal sentence

**Grammar Notes — Paragraph 2:**
- اخْتَارَتْ: feminine past tense form VIII (اِفْتَعَلَ) of خ-ي-ر, meaning "she chose"
- لِأَنَّ + pronoun: "because" — لِأَنَّهَا (because it/she) + فِعْل مَاضِي
- تَشْجِيعِ: مَصْدَر (verbal noun) of شَجَّعَ, meaning "encouragement"; after لِـ it takes genitive (مَجْرُور)
- عَاصِمَةً: accusative predicate of تَسْمِيَة (naming), indefinite (نَكِرَة)
- الْقِرَاءَةِ: verbal noun (مَصْدَر) of قَرَأَ — "reading" (the act of)
- نَتِيجَةُ: noun meaning "result"; subject of nominal sentence (مُبْتَدَأ)
- تَعَاوُنٍ: verbal noun (مَصْدَر) of تَعَاوَنَ, form VI of ع-و-ن — "cooperation"; genitive after نَتِيجَةُ (مُضَاف إِلَيْهِ)
- تَقَدُّمًا: verbal noun (مَصْدَر) of تَقَدَّمَ, form V of ق-د-م — "progress"; accusative object of تَشْهَدُ

**Grammar Notes — Paragraph 3:**
- تَخْتَصِرُ: Form VIII of خ-ص-ر — "she summarizes" — reflexive-passive pattern
- سَتُنَظِّمُ: future tense with سَـ prefix + form II present tense of ن-ظ-م (to organize)
- هِيَ: feminine pronoun referring back to الْفَعَالِيَّاتُ (the activities)
- مُحَارَبَةَ: verbal noun (مَصْدَر) of حَارَبَ, form III of ح-ر-ب — "fighting"
- لَقَدْ: emphasis particle used with the past tense (تَوْكِيد) — "indeed"
- كَانَ: past tense of "to be" (ك-و-ن), used to state a past fact
- حَقِيقَةُ: noun meaning "truth, fact" — subject of nominal sentence (مُبْتَدَأ)
- فُرْصَةٌ: noun meaning "opportunity" — indefinite (نَكِرَة) predicate
- تَجْرِبَةٌ: noun meaning "experience" — from ج-ر-ب; subject of nominal sentence
- يَقْرَأُ: present tense of قَرَأَ — "he reads"; relative clause describing مُجْتَمَعٍ

## Grammar Points

| # | Grammar Point | Arabic Example | Explanation |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| 1 | إِنَّ + accusative (الْمَفْعُول بِهِ) | إِنَّ رَبَاطَ هِيَ عَاصِمَةُ... | The particle إِنَّ puts the subject in accusative case: رَبَاطَ (not رَبَاطُ). Its predicate (خَبَر) remains nominative. |
| 2 | Feminine past tense verb | قَالَتْ / أَعْلَنَتْ / اخْتَارَتْ | Third-person feminine singular adds تَاء (ـتْ) to the verb stem: قَالَ → قَالَتْ. |
| 3 | Form II present tense pattern | تُنَظِّمُ / سَتُنَظِّمُ | Form II doubles the middle root letter and uses the يَفَعِّلُ pattern: نَظَّمَ → يُنَظِّمُ → تُنَظِّمُ. |
| 4 | Future tense with سَـ | سَتُنَظِّمُ | The prefix سَـ attached to the present tense indicates future: تُنَظِّمُ (she organizes) → سَتُنَظِّمُ (she will organize). |
| 5 | Verbal noun (المَصْدَر) after لِـ | لِتَشْجِيعِ الْقِرَاءَةِ | The preposition لِـ requires the genitive case. When followed by a مَصْدَر, it creates purpose: "to encourage reading." |
| 6 | Superlative (إِسْم تَفْضِيل) | الأَكْبَرَ / الثَّالِثَ | Pattern أَفْعَلُ for comparative/superlative: كَبِيرٌ → أَكْبَرُ (bigger / biggest). |
| 7 | Ordinal numbers | الثَّامِنِ / السَّادِسَةُ / الثَّالِثَ | Ordinals 1–10 follow the أَفْعَلُ/فَاعِل pattern; from 11 onward, use cardinal form + context. |
| 8 | لَقَدْ + past tense (تَوْكِيد) | لَقَدْ بَدَأَتِ الْاحْتِفَالِيَّاتُ | لَقَدْ adds emphasis to the past tense, meaning "indeed" or "certainly." It always precedes a past-tense verb. |
| 9 | Form VIII reflexive/passive | اخْتَارَتْ / تَخْتَصِرُ | Form VIII adds اِسْتَـ/اِخْتَـ to the stem, often giving a reflexive meaning: تَخْتَصِرُ = "she summarizes" (the text shortens itself). |
| 10 | Accusative of time (ظَرْف زَمَانِ) | يَوْمَ الثَّلَاثَاءِ / يَوْمَ الْخَمِيسِ | Time expressions use the accusative case (مَنْصُوب) when answering "when?": يَوْمَ (on the day of). |
| 11 | مُنْذُ + genitive (مَجْرُور) | مُنْذُ بَدْءِ هَذَا الْبَرْنَامَجِ | The preposition مُنْذُ (since) requires the following noun to be in genitive case: بَدْءِ (not بَدْءُ). |
| 12 | لِأَنَّ + pronoun suffix + verb | لِأَنَّهَا تَمْلِكُ... | لِأَنَّ (because) is a compound particle; the هَا pronoun suffix refers back to the feminine noun (Rabat/city). |
