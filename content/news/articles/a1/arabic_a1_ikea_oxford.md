## Grammar Points / نِقَاطُ الْقَوَاعِدِ

| # | Grammar Point | Arabic Name | Explanation | Example |
|---|-------------|-------------|-------------|---------|
| 1 | Demonstrative "This" | أَسْمَاءُ الْإِشَارَةِ | Use هَذَا (hādhā) before masculine nouns to say "this." It stays the same always. | هَذَا مَتْجَرٌ (This is a shop) |
| 2 | Preposition فِي | حَرْفُ الْجَرِّ فِي | فِي means "in." The word after it changes to have كَسْرَة (i sound) at the end. | فِي مَدِينَةِ (in a city), فِي شَارِعِ (in a street) |
| 3 | Present Tense Verbs | الْفِعْلُ الْمُضَارِعُ | Arabic verbs start with يَـ or نُـ for "he" or "we." This shows the action is happening now. | يَبِيعُ (he sells), نُرِيدُ (we want) |
| 4 | Adjective After Noun | النَّعْتُ | The adjective comes AFTER the noun in Arabic. It must match: same gender, same case. | مَتْجَرٌ جَدِيدٌ (a new store), طَعَامٌ لَذِيذٌ (delicious food) |
| 5 | The Word أَوْ | أَدَاةُ الْعَطْفِ أَوْ | أَوْ means "or." It connects two choices together. | نُقُودًا أَوْ بِطَاقَةٍ (cash or card) |
| 6 | Construct (Idafa) | الْإِضَافَةُ | Put two nouns together to show "of" or possession. The second noun loses الْ. | كُرَاتُ اللَّحْمِ (meatballs = balls of meat) |

## Source / الْمَصْدَرُ

> This article is based on real news events. IKEA opened its new Oxford Street store at 214 Oxford Street, London on **May 1, 2025**. The store occupies the former Topshop building with 3 floors and 5,800 square metres, selling 3,500 items. The Swedish deli features 130 seats with meatballs at £4.95 and hot dogs at £0.75. The store received 3,730 job applications in 5 days for 150 positions. Opening hours are 10am–8pm daily. Quote attributed to Peter Jelkeby, IKEA UK CEO.
