## 📐 Grammar Points

| # | Grammar Point | Arabic Example | Plain | Explanation |
|---|---------------|----------------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | **Verb-Subject-Object (VSO)** | أَقَامَ النَّاسُ مَهْرَجَانًا | أقام الناس مهرجانًا | Arabic puts the **verb first**, then **subject**, then **object**. English puts subject first: "The people held a festival." |
| 2 | **Adjective comes after noun** | مِقْلَاةٌ **كَبِيرَةٌ** | مقلاة **كبيرة** | The adjective **follows** the noun. Also: both must match in gender (feminine ـة). |
| 3 | **The Construct / إِضَافَة** | عَرْضُ **الْمِقْلَاةِ** | عرض **المقلاة** | Noun + Noun possession: "the pan's width." First word loses الْ. Second word gets الْ + kasra (i). |
| 4 | **Preposition فِي** | فِي الْمَكْسِيكَ | في المكسيك | فِي = "in". The word after فِي gets kasra (i): المكسيكَ → المكسي**كَ**. |
| 5 | **Preposition لِـ (for/to)** | لِلْأَيْتَامِ | للأيتام | لِـ + الْ = لِلْ (for the / to the). The word after gets kasra (i): الأيتامِ. |
| 6 | **Past tense "they" verb** | أَكَلُوا / أَعْطَوْا / طَبَخُوا | أكلوا / أعطوا / طبخوا | "They" past tense ends in ـوا. These are all **majrūr** (unchanged) for "they." |
| 7 | **هَذَا (this)** | هَذَا رَقْمٌ قِيَاسِيٌّ | هذا رقم قياسي | هَذَا = "this" (for masculine nouns). Use هَذِهِ for feminine nouns. |
| 8 | **Numbers 11-19** | ثَلَاثَةَ عَشْرَ أَلْفَ | ثلاثة عشر ألف | Numbers 11-19: both parts match the counted noun in case. The counted noun is singular after أَلْف. |

## 📢 The Quote

> **Arabic:** وَصَفَتْ سُوزَانَا رَايِيسَ — مُدَقِّقَةُ جِينِيس: "كَانَ رَائِعًا كَيْفَ جَمَعَ الْمُنَظِّمُونَ بَيْنَ رُوحِ الْمُجْتَمَعِ وَالْفَخْرِ الثَّقَافِيِّ وَالنَّارِ وَالطَّعْمِ."
> **Plain:** وصفت سوزانا راييس — مدققة جينيس: "كان رائعًا كيف جمع المنظمون بين روح المجتمع والفخر الثقافي والنار والطعم."
> **English:** Guinness adjudicator Susana Reyes said: "It was wonderful how the organizers blended community spirit and cultural pride and fire and flavour."

**Who is Susana?** Susana Reyes is an adjudicator from Guinness World Records. She came to Mexico to watch the festival. She said it was wonderful how the organizers blended community spirit, cultural pride, fire, and flavour.

## 🗺️ About the Place

**Guadalupe, Nuevo León, Mexico**

- **Guadalupe** (غُوَادَلُوبَ) is a city near Monterrey in northern Mexico.
- **Nuevo León** is a state in Mexico known for its mountains and delicious food.
- **La Discada** is a traditional Mexican dish cooked in a big pan with meat, vegetables, and spices.
