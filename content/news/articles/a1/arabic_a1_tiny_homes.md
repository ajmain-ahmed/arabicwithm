## Grammar Points

| # | Grammar Point | Arabic Example | Plain Example | Simple Explanation |
|---|--------------|--------------|--------------|-------------------|
| 1 | **Definite article الْـ** | الْبُيُوتُ / الْحَاكِمُ | البيوت / الحاكم | **الْـ** = "the". It connects to the word. Any word with الْـ is specific and known. |
| 2 | **Preposition لِ = "for"** | لِلْجُنُودِ / لِلْمَشْرُوعِ | للجنود / للمشروع | **لِ + الْـ = لِلْـ** = "for the". It shows who receives something. |
| 3 | **Negation with لَيْسَ** | لَيْسَ لَدَيْهِمْ مَنَازِلُ | ليس لديهم منازل | **لَيْسَ** = "there is not / is not". It makes a sentence negative. It goes with the word after it. |
| 4 | **Adjective-noun agreement** | بَيْتًا صَغِيرًا | بيتا صغيرا | When a noun gets a marker (like ًا), the adjective next to it gets the **same marker**. They must match! |
| 5 | **Numbers 3-10: reverse agreement** | أَرْبَعِينَ بَيْتًا / اثْنَانِ وَثَلَاثُونَ أَلْفًا | أربعين بيتا / اثنان وثلاثون ألفا | Numbers 3-10 use **opposite gender** with nouns. Feminine number + masculine noun, or masculine number + feminine noun. |

## Source

**Original Story:** Veterans Community Project builds 40 tiny homes for homeless veterans in Milwaukee, Wisconsin. Construction began October 2025. Each home is 240-320 sq ft (22-30 m²) with a bed, kitchen, and bathroom. Rent is $0. Total project cost: $11.7 million ($2.5M from state). The village sits on 7 acres. 85% of residents stay an average of 335 days. CEO Bryan Meyer: "Veterans pay zero in rent." Governor Tony Evers: "A safe place to sleep and a chance to heal." Over 32,000 veterans are homeless in the United States.
