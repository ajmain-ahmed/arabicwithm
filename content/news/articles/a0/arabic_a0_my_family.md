## Grammar Points

| # | Pattern | How It Works | Example |
|---|---------|-------------|---------|
| 1 | **هَذَا / هَذِهِ** (This is) | Use **هَذَا** for masculine things/people. Use **هَذِهِ** for feminine things/people. | هَذَا أَبِي (This is my father.) هَذِهِ أُمِّي (This is my mother.) |
| 2 | **ـي = my** | Add **ـي** to the end of a noun to mean "my." It sticks right onto the word. | أَب + ـي = أَبِي (my father) أُسْرَة + ـي = أُسْرَتِي (my family) |
| 3 | **ـنَا = our** | Add **ـنَا** to the end of a noun to mean "our." | أُسْرَة + ـنَا = أُسْرَتَنَا (our family) |
| 4 | **هُوَ / هِيَ / نَحْنُ** | **هُوَ** = he (one man/boy). **هِيَ** = she (one woman/girl). **نَحْنُ** = we (me + others). | هُوَ رَجُلٌ (He is a man.) هِيَ امْرَأَةٌ (She is a woman.) نَحْنُ فِي الْبَيْتِ (We are in the house.) |
| 5 | **Matching adjectives** | Adjectives must "match" the person they describe. For a male, the adjective has no special ending. For a female, add **ـَة** to the adjective. | أَخِي الصَّغِيرُ (my little brother) أُخْتِي الصَّغِيرَةُ (my little sister) |
