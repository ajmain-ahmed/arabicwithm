## Grammar Points

| # | Pattern | How It Works | Example |
|---|---------|-------------|---------|
| 1 | **الْـ = the** | Put **الْـ** at the start of a noun to mean "the." | الْمَاءُ = the water, الشَّمْسُ = the sun, النَّاسُ = the people |
| 2 | **لَا + verb = negative** | Put **لَا** before a verb to say "do not" or "does not." | لَا تَلْعَبُوا = do not play, لَا يَجْرِي = it does not blow |
| 3 | **ـونَ = they** | The ending **ـونَ** on a verb means "they" (a group). | يَشْرَبُونَ = they drink, يَجْلِسُونَ = they sit, يَعْمَلُونَ = they work |
| 4 | **لِـ = for** | Put **لِـ** before a noun to mean "for." | لِلنَّاسِ = for the people, لِلْأَطْفَالِ = for the children, لِلرَّاحَةِ = for rest |
| 5 | **ـَتْ = she (past)** | The ending **ـَتْ** on a past verb means "she." | فَتَحَتْ = she opened, قَالَتْ = she said |
| 6 | **Matching adjectives (feminine)** | When describing a feminine noun, add **ـَةٌ** to the adjective. | الشَّمْسُ قَوِيَّةٌ (the sun is strong), الْمَرَاكِزُ الْبَارِدَةُ (the cool centers) |

*Article adapted from Associated Press, March 12, 2026, and FOX 11 Los Angeles, March 17, 2026. On March 12, temperatures in Los Angeles reached 35°C (95°F) — about 20 degrees above the normal March temperature of 21°C (70°F). Mayor Karen Bass opened hundreds of cooling centers and urged residents to drink water and stay indoors. The heat wave was caused by a high-pressure system and a lack of cooling Pacific Ocean winds.*
