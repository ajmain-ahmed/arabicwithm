## Grammar Points

| Grammar Point | Arabic Example | English Explanation |
|--------------|----------------|---------------------|
| **Verb — Present Tense** | يَحْمِلُ, يَأْخُذُ, يَمْشِي | These are present tense verbs. They start with **يـ** = "he." يَحْمِلُ = he carries. |
| **Verb — Present Tense (They)** | يَرَوْنَ, يَفْرَحُونَ | Verbs with **ـونَ** at the end = "they." يَفْرَحُونَ = they become happy. |
| **Adjective Agreement** | كَلْبٌ صَغِيرٌ, كَلْبٌ سَعِيدٌ | The adjective comes AFTER the noun. Both have the same ending. |
| **The Word "وَ" (and)** | صَغِيرٌ وَسَعِيدٌ | وَ = "and." It connects two words together. |
| **Definite Article "الْ"** | الْكِلَابُ, النَّاسُ, الْمَدِينَةُ | الْ = "the." It makes a noun specific. |
| **Preposition "فِي" (in)** | فِي الْمَدِينَةِ, فِي الْحَدِيقَةِ | فِي = "in." It comes BEFORE the noun. |
| **Preposition "مِنْ" (from)** | مِنَ الْمَلْجَأِ | مِنْ = "from." It shows where something comes from. |
| **Sentence Order** | بْرَايَانُ يَحْمِلُ الْكَلْبَ. | Arabic order: **Person + Verb + Object.** Bryan carries the dog. |
