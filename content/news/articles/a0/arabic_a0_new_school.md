## Grammar Points

| Grammar Point | Arabic Example | Explanation | Simple Rule |
|---------------|--------------|-------------|-------------|
| **Definite Article الـ** | **الْ**مَدْرَسَةِ, **الْ**مَبْنَى | The word "the" in Arabic is **الْ** | Add **الْ** to the beginning of a noun to make it "the ..." |
| **Noun-Adjective Agreement** | مَدْرَسَةٌ **جَدِيدَةٌ** | School (fem.) + New (fem.) | Adjective must match the noun: feminine with feminine, masculine with masculine |
| **Passive Verb** | **اِفْتُتِحَتْ** الْمَدْرَسَةُ | "The school **was opened**" | When something receives the action, use the passive form with a **ُ** sound on the first letter |
| **Numbers 3-10** | **ثَلَاثَةُ** طَوَابِقَ | "Three floors" | Numbers 3-10 have a special form; the noun after them is plural |
| **Idafa (Possession)** | **اسْمُ الْمَدْرَسَةِ** | "The school's name" / "Name of the school" | Put two nouns together: the first has no "al-", the second has "al-" with kasra ending |
| **Plural Adjective** | النَّاسُ **سُعَدَاءُ** | "The people are happy" | Some adjectives have a special plural form; سُعَدَاءُ is the plural of سَعِيدٌ |
| **Preposition + Pronoun** | **فِيهَا**, **فِيهِ**, **بِـ** | "in it" (fem./masc.), "with/by" | When you add a pronoun like "it" after a preposition, it sticks to the preposition |
| **Ordinal Numbers** | **الْأَوَّلَ** | "the first" | To say "first, second, third," use a special form of the number |

## Did You Know? 💡

- **Falling Creek Middle School** cost **$103 million** to build — that's more than one hundred million dollars!
- The school has **239,000 square feet** of space. It is the **biggest middle school** in central Virginia.
- The gym seats **1,200** people. The auditorium seats **1,000** people. The cafeteria seats **650** people.
- The school has **three floors** and opened on **August 19, 2024** for the first day of classes.
- **53%** of the students are boys and **47%** are girls.
- Dominique Chatters, a school board member, **cried tears of joy** when she walked through the building!
