## Grammar Points

| # | Grammar Point | Explanation | Example from Article |
|---|--------------|-------------|---------------------|
| 1 | **No "is/are" in present tense** | Arabic does not use a word for "is" or "are" in the present tense. Just put two words together! | هِيَ حَدِيقَةُ المَرْفَأِ = "It [is] Pier park" |
| 2 | **الـ = "the"** | Putting الـ at the beginning of a word makes it "the." | الحَدِيقَةُ = the park; المَاءُ = the water |
| 3 | **Feminine ending: ة** | Words that end in ـة are usually feminine. The describing word must also end in ـة. | حَدِيقَةٌ جَدِيدَةٌ (a new park); حَدِيقَةٌ كَبِيرَةٌ (a big park) |
| 4 | **فِي + kasra** | The preposition فِي ("in") gives the next word a kasra (ـِ) sound at the end. | فِي نِيُويُورْكَ; فِي الحَدِيقَةِ |
| 5 | **مِنَ + kasra** | The preposition مِنْ ("from/of") gives the next word kasra. Before الـ it becomes مِنَ. | مِنْ يُولْيُو; مِنَ المَاءِ |
| 6 | **لِ + "for"** | لِ means "for." When combined with الـ it becomes لِلْـ. | لِلأَطْفَالِ (for the children); لِلتِّنِسِ (for tennis) |
| 7 | **Dual = ـَانِ (two things)** | To say "two" of something, add ـَانِ to the end of the noun. | مَلْعَبَانِ = two courts / playgrounds |
| 8 | **Action word before thing = no-doer form** | When the action word comes first, and there is no "doer," it means "was done." | فُتِحَتْ = was opened (no person is mentioned) |
| 9 | **فِي + هَا = فِيهَا** | Adding هَا ("it/she") to a preposition like فِي makes "in it." | فِيهَا مَلْعَبٌ = In it [is] a court |
| 10 | **Numbers 3-10 + plural noun** | After numbers 3-10, the noun is plural and follows the number. | أَرْبَعَةُ مَلاَعِبَ = four courts |
| 11 | **Subject ending (damma)** | The subject of a sentence has damma (ـُ) or double damma (ـٌ) at the end. | حَدِيقَةٌ; حَدِيقَةُ; العَمَلُ |
| 12 | **Object ending (fatha)** | The object of an action word has fatha (ـَ) or double fatha (ـً) at the end. | أَهْلاً; الحَدِيقَةَ; يَوْمَ |
| 13 | **After preposition ending (kasra)** | After prepositions like فِي, مِنْ, لِ, the word has kasra (ـِ). | الحَدِيقَةِ; المَاءِ; يُولْيُو |
| 14 | **سُو = "Sue"** | Names of people stay the same in all cases. No changes at the end! | قَالَتْ سُو (Sue said) |
| 15 | **وَ = "and"** | وَ connects two words or two sentences. Just say "wa" and keep going! | جَمِيلَةٌ وَكَبِيرَةٌ; نُحِبُّ الحَدِيقَةَ وَنَحْنُ سُعَدَاءُ |

## Additional Real Facts (Did You Know?)

- The park cost **$33.6 million** to build (تَكْلِفَةُ الحَدِيقَةِ ثَلَاثَةٌ وَثَلَاثُونَ مِلْيُونًا وَسِتُّ مِئَاتِ أَلْفِ دُولَارٍ).
- Two NYC Council Members attended: **Carlina Rivera** and **Christopher Marte** (كَرْلِينَا رِيفِيرَا وَكْرِيسْتُوفَرُ مَارْتِي).
- The park transformed an **old parking lot and warehouse** into green space (مِنْ مَوْقِفٍ قَدِيمٍ وَمَخْزَنٍ إِلَى مَكَانٍ أَخْضَرَ).
- The opening ceremony included a **ribbon-cutting** on July 3, 2024 (قَصَّتِ الشَّرِيطَ يَوْمَ الثَّالِثِ مِنْ يُولْيُو).
- Before this park, the area was an **empty parking lot and a warehouse** for many years.
