## Script

---

### 0:00 — Speed, I'm not joking.

**Arabic:** سَرِيعُ.. لَسْتُ أَمْزَحُ.  
**Plain:** سريع.. لست أمزح.

| Arabic | Transliteration | English | CEFR |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| سَرِيعُ | sarīʿu | Speed | A1 |
| لَسْتُ | lastu | I'm not | A1 |
| أَمْزَحُ | ʾamzaḥu | I joke | A2 |

**Note:** *لَسْتُ* = a negation word ("I am not"). It always goes before a description or action word.  
**Note:** *أَمْزَحُ* = present tense verb. The *أَ* at the start = "I" (built into the verb).

---

### 0:01 — Show yourself immediately.

**Arabic:** أَظْهِرْ نَفْسَكَ عَلَى الْفَوْرِ.  
**Plain:** أظهر نفسك على الفور.

| Arabic | Transliteration | English | CEFR |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| أَظْهِرْ | ʾaẓhir | show! | A2 |
| نَفْسَكَ | nafsaka | yourself | A2 |
| عَلَى الْفَوْرِ | ʿalā l-fawri | immediately | B1 |

**Note:** *نَفْسَكَ* = *نَفْس* (self) + *ـكَ* (your). Add *ـكَ* to make it "yourself."  
**Note:** *عَلَى الْفَوْرِ* = a set expression meaning "right away." Learn it as a phrase.

---

### 0:04 — Alright, get down now.

**Arabic:** حَسَنًا.. أَنْزِلِ الْآنَ.  
**Plain:** حسنا.. أنزل الآن.

| Arabic | Transliteration | English | CEFR |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| حَسَنًا | ḥasanan | alright / okay | A1 |
| أَنْزِلِ | ʾanzili | get down! | B1 |
| الْآنَ | al-āna | now | A1 |

---

### 0:07 — I'm taking the ball.

**Arabic:** سَآخُذُ الْكُرَةَ.  
**Plain:** سآخذ الكرة.

| Arabic | Transliteration | English | CEFR |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| سَآخُذُ | saʾākhudhu | I will take | A2 |
| الْكُرَةَ | al-kurata | the ball | A1 |

**Note:** *سَـ* at the start of a verb = future tense ("will ___"). Just put *سَ* before any present tense verb to make it future.

---

### 0:10 — Speed, how dare you?!

**Arabic:** سَرِيعُ.. كَيْفَ تَجْرُؤُ؟!  
**Plain:** سريع.. كيف تجرؤ؟!

| Arabic | Transliteration | English | CEFR |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| سَرِيعُ | sarīʿu | Speed | A1 |
| كَيْفَ تَجْرُؤُ | kayfa tajruʾu | how dare you | B1 |

**Note:** *كَيْفَ تَجْرُؤُ* = a set expression. Learn it as "how dare you!"

---

### 0:12 — You have to make a decision—

**Arabic:** عَلَيْكَ أَنْ تَتَّخِذَ قَرَارًا..  
**Plain:** عليك أن تتخذ قرارًا..

| Arabic | Transliteration | English | CEFR |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| عَلَيْكَ | ʿalayka | you must | A1 |
| أَنْ | an | to | A2 |
| تَتَّخِذَ | tattakhidha | you make / take | B1 |
| قَرَارًا | qarāran | a decision | A2 |

**Note:** *عَلَيْكَ أَنْ + verb* = "you must ___" / "you have to ___." Very common pattern!  
**Note:** *أَنْ* is a small word that changes the verb ending to *ـَ* (instead of *ـُ*). It connects "you must" to the action.

---

### 0:14 — It's either me or the ball.

**Arabic:** إِمَّا أَنَا.. أَوْ الْكُرَةُ.  
**Plain:** إما أنا.. أو الكرة.

| Arabic | Transliteration | English | CEFR |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| إِمَّا... أَوْ | ʾimmā... aw | either... or | A2 |
| أَنَا | anā | I / me | A1 |
| الْكُرَةُ | al-kuratu | the ball | A1 |

### 0:18 — Fine. I wish you happiness without me.

**Arabic:** حَسَنًا.. أَتَمَنَّى لَكُمُ السَّعَادَةَ.. بِدُونِي.  
**Plain:** حسنا.. أتمنى لكم السعادة.. بدوني.

| Arabic | Transliteration | English | CEFR |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| حَسَنًا | ḥasanan | fine / alright | A1 |
| أَتَمَنَّى | ʾatamannā | I wish | A2 |
| لَكُمُ | la-kumu | for you (pl.) | A1 |
| السَّعَادَةَ | as-saʿādah | happiness | A1 |
| بِدُونِي | bi-dūnī | without me | A2 |

**Note:** *بِدُونِي* = *بِدُون* (without) + *ـي* (me). Add *ـي* to make it "without me."

## Grammar Points

| # | Pattern | How It Works | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **سَـ + verb** | Put *سَ* before any present verb to make it future ("will ___") | سَآخُذُ = I will take |
| 2 | **عَلَيْكَ أَنْ + verb** | "You must ___" — very common for giving instructions | عَلَيْكَ أَنْ تَتَّخِذَ = You have to make |
| 3 | **Imperative** | Commands — just say the verb form directly to someone | أَظْهِرْ = show! / أَنْزِلِ = get down! |
| 4 | **لَسْتُ** | "I am not" — a fixed negation word | لَسْتُ أَمْزَحُ = I'm not joking |
| 5 | **نَفْس + suffix** | Add a pronoun suffix to *نَفْس* to make "___self" | نَفْسَكَ = yourself |
| 6 | **إِمَّا... أَوْ** | "Either... or" — presents two choices | إِمَّا أَنَا أَوْ الْكُرَةُ = either me or the ball |
