### 0:05 — There is no power nor strength except through Allah. Who do we have after you, O Farooq?

**Arabic:** لَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللَّهِ. مَنْ لَنَا بَعْدَكَ يَا فَارُوق؟

### 0:10 — May Allah save you, O Commander of the Faithful. May Allah save you.

**Arabic:** نَجَّاكَ اللَّهُ يَا أَمِيرَ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ، نَجَّاكَ اللَّهُ.  
**Plain:** نجاك الله يا أمير المؤمنين، نجاك الله.

| Arabic | Transliteration | English | CEFR |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| نَجَّاكَ | najjāka | (May He) save you | B1 |
| اللَّهُ | Allāhu | Allah | A1 |
| يَا | yā | O | A1 |
| أَمِيرَ | amīra | commander | A2 |
| الْمُؤْمِنِينَ | al-muʾminīna | the believers | A2 |

**Note:** *نَجَّاكَ* = verb + "you" glued together. Literally: "May He save-you." Arabic puts the prayer/hope first, then the person.

**Note:** *أَمِيرَ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ* = "Commander of the Believers" — a famous title. The second word gets a special *ـِينَ* ending to link to the first.

**Plain:** نعم يا أبي، لقد أتممنا الصلاة.

| Arabic | Transliteration | English | CEFR |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| نَعَمْ | naʿam | yes | A1 |
| يَا | yā | O | A1 |
| أَبِي | abī | my father | A1 |
| لَقَدْ | laqad | certainly / indeed | A2 |
| أَتْمَمْنَا | ʾatmamnā | we completed | B1 |
| الصَّلَاةَ | al-ṣalāta | the prayer | A1 |

**Note:** *الصَّلَاةَ* has an *ـَ* ending here because it's the thing **being finished** — receiving the action.

### 1:26 — "And not a Muslim holding a grudge against me, who would argue against me before my Lord. Indeed we belong to Allah, and to Him we return."

### 1:35 — May Allah have mercy on you, father, and reunite me with you in His Paradise by His grace and His forgiveness.

**Arabic:** رَحِمَكَ اللَّهُ يَا أَبِي، وَجَمَعَنِي بِكَ فِي جَنَّتِهِ بِفَضْلِهِ وَمَغْفِرَتِهِ.  
**Plain:** رحمك الله يا أبي، وجمعني بك في جنته بفضله ومغفرته.

| Arabic | Transliteration | English | CEFR |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| رَحِمَكَ | raḥimaka | [may He have] mercy on you | A2 |
| اللَّهُ | Allāhu | Allah | A1 |
| يَا | yā | O | A1 |
| أَبِي | abī | my father | A1 |
| وَ | wa | and | A1 |
| جَمَعَنِي | jamaʿanī | gather me / reunite me | A2 |
| بِكَ | bika | with you | A2 |
| فِي | fī | in | A1 |
| جَنَّتِهِ | jannatihi | His Paradise | A2 |
| بِفَضْلِهِ | bi-faḍlihi | by His grace | A2 |
| وَ | wa | and | A1 |
| مَغْفِرَتِهِ | maghfiratihi | His forgiveness | A2 |

**Note:** *رَحِمَكَ اللَّهُ* = word order is flipped from English. Literally: "[have mercy on]-you, Allah." Arabic puts the prayer action first, then the person, then who is doing it.  
**Note:** *جَنَّتِهِ* / *بِفَضْلِهِ* / *مَغْفِرَتِهِ* — the *ـهِ* ending = "his" (all referring to Allah). The "i" sound links the words in a chain.

## Grammar Points

| # | Pattern | How It Works | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **Prayer / hope verb-first** | Put the verb first when praying or hoping. The person comes after, with a suffix. | نَجَّاكَ اللَّهُ = "May He save you" (lit. "save-you, Allah") |
| 2 | **كَانَ + present verb** | "He was" + present verb = past habit: "he **used to** ___" | كَانَ يُفَرِّقُ = he used to distinguish |
| 3 | **Passive verb** | Change the verb shape to show the person **received** the action (not who did it). | طُعِنَ = he was stabbed |
| 4 | **لَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللَّهِ** | Fixed Islamic phrase. Learn as one block. | — |
| 5 | **غَيْر + noun** | "Other than" + noun = "non-[noun]" | غَيْرَ مُسْلِمٍ = non-Muslim |
| 6 | **لَقَدْ + past verb** | Adds strong emphasis: "We **definitely** did…" | لَقَدْ أَتْمَمْنَا = we definitely completed |
| 7 | **Doubled verb = intensity** | Doubling the middle letter makes the meaning stronger. | أَتْمَمْنَا = we completed **fully** |
| 8 | **Verb + pronoun suffix** | "Me / you / him / them" sticks to the end of verbs. | طَعَنَهُ = stabbed him / طَعَنَنِي = stabbed me / سَأَلَهُمْ = asked them |
| 9 | **Noun + pronoun suffix** | "My / your / his / our" sticks to the end of nouns. | قَلْبِهِ = his heart / إِنْصَافِهِ = his justice |
| 10 | **لَيْسَ + noun/adjective** | "It is not." Changes the next word's ending to *ـًا*. | لَيْسَ مُسْلِمًا = (he) is not a Muslim |
| 11 | **إِنَّا لِلَّهِ…** | The famous phrase for death/loss. Memorize as one block. | إِنَّا لِلَّهِ وَإِنَّا إِلَيْهِ رَاجِعُونَ = Indeed we belong to Allah… |
| 12 | **رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ** | Respectful phrase after a righteous person's name. | رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ = may Allah be pleased with him |
| 13 | **Word order: action → person → doer** | Arabic often flips English order for prayers. | رَحِمَكَ اللَّهُ = [have mercy]-you, Allah |
| 14 | **Adjectives stack after the noun** | Multiple adjectives come after the noun, each with *الـ*. | الْكَافِرُ الْحَاقِدُ = the resentful disbeliever |
